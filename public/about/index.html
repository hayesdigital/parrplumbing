Local roots, licensed work

Greg Parr has lived in Ponca City his entire life. He's been a licensed Oklahoma plumbing contractor for over a decade, and Parr Plumbing is how he takes care of his neighbors' plumbing, one house and one business at a time.

Over the years, water heaters became Greg's specialty. Repairing them, replacing them and helping people figure out which one makes sense. It's the kind of job where honest advice matters, because the difference between a repair and a replacement can be a lot of money.

How we work

  • Straight answers. We tell you what's wrong and what it'll cost before we start.
  • Repair first when it makes sense. We won't sell you a new water heater you don't need.
  • Done right. Licensed, insured and installed to code.
  • Respect for your home. We clean up when we're done.
