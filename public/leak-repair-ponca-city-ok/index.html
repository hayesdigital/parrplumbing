A leak is never just a little water

That small drip under the sink? It's been going longer than you think. Leaks have a way of hiding until there's a stain on the ceiling, a soft spot in the floor or a water bill that makes you do a double take.

Parr Plumbing finds and fixes leaks in kitchens, bathrooms and underground lines all over Ponca City. We track down where the water's actually coming from, fix it properly and help you stop the damage before it spreads.

Leaks we fix

  • Under sink leaksSupply lines, drain traps, shutoff valves and faucets.
  • Bathroom leaksToilets, tub and shower valves, and vanity plumbing.
  • Water heater leaksFittings, relief valves and drain valves.
  • Underground line leaksWater lines between the meter and your home.
  • Dripping faucetsThat steady drip adds up on your water bill.
  • Running toiletsOne of the biggest hidden water wasters in a home.

Signs of a hidden plumbing leak

  • Your water bill went up and your habits didn't change
  • You hear water running when every faucet is off
  • Water stains on ceilings or walls
  • Warped, soft or stained flooring
  • A musty smell or mold in cabinets
  • Wet or extra green spots in the yard over your water line
  • The water meter keeps spinning with everything off

Got a leak right now? Do this first

Find the closest shutoff valve and turn it off. Most sinks and toilets have one right underneath. If you can't stop it there, shut off the main water valve to the house.

Move anything valuable out of the way, soak up what you can and snap a few photos for your records. Then call (580) 304-4967 and we'll take it from there.

FAQ

Leak Repair questions

How do I know if I have a hidden water leak?

Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, the sound of running water when everything's off, damp spots on floors or walls, a musty smell or a water meter that keeps moving when no water is in use.

What should I do first if I have a leak?

Shut off the water. Use the valve under the sink or behind the toilet if the leak is there, or the main shutoff for the house if you can't isolate it. Then call us.

Do you repair underground water line leaks?

Yes. We find and fix leaks in kitchens, bathrooms and underground lines.

Can a small drip really cause damage?

It can. A slow drip under a sink or behind a wall can rot cabinets, warp floors and grow mold over time. It's cheaper to fix early.

Is my water heater leaking?

If there's water around the base of your water heater, check our water heater repair page. Some leaks are an easy fix and some mean the tank needs replacing.
